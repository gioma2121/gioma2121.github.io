01
Honors Research · ongoing/Interpretability · World models

Latents Audit & Language Extension of DreamZero

My Honors Academy research project at TU/e, supervised by Bram Grooten (AMOR/e lab, TU/e). DreamZero is a world action model that predicts video and actions jointly. I'm auditing what its internal representations actually encode, on LIBERO manipulation tasks, and extending it towards language so the model can describe what it sees and expects. The work runs on Snellius, the Dutch national supercomputer. Three research questions drive it:

  1. RQ1 · Readout. What does a frozen DreamZero know about the present, the next transition and the final outcome, and can a grounded language readout describe it faithfully, beyond what priors and structured outputs already give?
  2. RQ2 · Monitoring. Does disagreement between stochastic samples from the same context add useful information for predicting failure?
  3. RQ3 · Reasoning & control. Can a unified language output support planning or intervention, with causal evidence that it helps?

03
Course project · 2AMM40/ML for Science

Neural Surrogates for Physical Simulation

Group project for Machine Learning for Science (TU/e): learning fast surrogates of physical systems, in four phases, from deterministic networks to probabilistic ones.

  1. Eulerian dynamics. Learning 2D Navier–Stokes on a fixed grid. A periodic U-Net and a full Transformer as baselines, then a Transolver-style physics-slice Transformer that compresses 1024 grid points into 64 learned slices. I also built pushforward training with an adaptive difficulty scheduler: slightly worse on single steps, clearly better on long rollouts.
  2. Lagrangian dynamics. 100 flocking boids in 3D, modelled with an equivariant graph network (EGNN) on a kNN graph, with an added alignment term, velocity-noise training and rollout-based checkpoint selection.
  3. Flow matching on meshes. CylinderFlow on a non-uniform mesh, with a MeshGraphNets-style processor plus global attention. An MSE model learns the conditional mean and its wake dies out; conditional flow matching keeps the vortex shedding alive.
  4. Pedestrians at Eindhoven Centraal. A spatio-temporal graph model generating 20 future frames at once, where a horizon-aware informed prior beat a plain Gaussian prior on all four trajectory metrics.

04
Course project/Knowledge Engineering

GreenGrid NL: Renewable Siting via Knowledge Graph

Consulting-style project: which Dutch municipalities are best suited for wind and solar, and where do Natura 2000 conflicts bind? I built a pipeline that scores every municipality for wind (Global Wind Atlas) and solar (PVOUT), measures conflict as spatial overlap with protected areas, and adds grid distance and population density. Everything lands in a Neo4j knowledge graph of municipalities, provinces, Natura 2000 sites, grid segments and technologies, validated against competency questions. A three-page Dash dashboard (map, analytics, and natural-language-to-Cypher queries) lets a client explore it.

08
BSc Thesis · 109/110/Time series

Macrofinancial Effects of US Political Cycles

Can deep learning surface how US political cycles shape macrofinancial dynamics? I built and compared Temporal Fusion Transformer and LSTM models on a 40-year daily dataset of economic, financial, and sentiment indicators. TFT's attention is genuinely suited to multi-horizon forecasting, then I interpreted it via native attention weights and LIME to surface regime-dependent patterns. That interpretability layer is what made the thesis worth reading.
